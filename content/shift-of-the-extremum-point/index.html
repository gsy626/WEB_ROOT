一道极值点偏移问题

g(x) = -1 - x + \ln{x} + t

g(x_1) = g(x_2) = 0

证明：x_1 + x_2 > t

证：

图：y = x - \ln{x} + 1 与 y = t

由 g(x) = 0 得：

-1 - x + \ln{x} + t = 0 \Leftrightarrow x + 1 - \ln{x} = t

如图1所示。

易得 t > 2 ，不妨设 0 < x_1 < 1 < x_2 。

\begin{aligned} & x_1 + x_2 > t \\ \Leftrightarrow & x_1 + x_2 > x_1 + 1 - \ln{x_1} \\ \Leftrightarrow & x_2 + \ln{x_1} > 1 \end{aligned}

设 x_1 = x_1(t),\; x_2 = x_2(t) ，h(t) = x_2 + \ln{x_1} ，则只需证 h(t) > 1 。

\begin{aligned} & g(x_1) = -1 - x_1 + \ln{x_1} + t = 0 \\ & g(x_2) = -1 - x_2 + \ln{x_2} + t = 0 \end{aligned}

即

\begin{aligned} & t = 1 + x_1 - \ln{x_1} \\ & t = 1 + x_2 - \ln{x_2} \end{aligned}

两侧对 t 求导：

\begin{aligned} & 1 = \left(1 - \frac{1}{x_1}\right) x_1'(t) \\ & 1 = \left(1 - \frac{1}{x_2}\right) x_2'(t) \end{aligned} \begin{aligned} & x_1'(t) = \frac{1}{1 - \frac{1}{x_1}} = \frac{x_1}{x_1 - 1} \\ & x_2'(t) = \frac{1}{1 - \frac{1}{x_2}} = \frac{x_2}{x_2 - 1} \end{aligned}

由 h(t) = x_2 + \ln{x_1} ，对 t 求导：

\begin{aligned} h'(t) &= x_2'(t) + \frac{x_1'(t)}{x_1} \\ &= \frac{x_2}{x_2 - 1} + \frac{\frac{x_1}{x_1 - 1}}{x_1} \\ &= \frac{x_2}{x_2 - 1} + \frac{1}{x_1 - 1} \\ &= \frac{x_1 x_2 - 1}{(x_1 - 1)(x_2 - 1)} \end{aligned}

已得 x_1 < 1 < x_2 ，下证 x_1 x_2 < 1 。

由

\begin{cases} t = 1 + x_1 - \ln{x_1} \\ t = 1 + x_2 - \ln{x_2} \end{cases}

上式减下式得：

x_2 - x_1 = \ln{x_2} - \ln{x_1} = \ln{\frac{x_2}{x_1}}

设 p = \frac{x_2}{x_1} > 1 ，

\begin{cases} x_2 - x_1 = \ln{p} \\ p = \frac{x_2}{x_1} \end{cases}

解得：

\begin{cases} x_1 = \frac{p}{p-1} \ln{p} \\ x_2 = \frac{1}{p-1} \ln{p} \end{cases} \begin{aligned} x_1 x_2 &= \frac{p}{(p-1)^2} \ln^2{p} \\ &= \left( \frac{\sqrt{p}}{p-1} \ln{p} \right)^2 \end{aligned}

下证 \frac{\sqrt{p}}{p-1} \ln{p} < 1 。

\begin{aligned} & \frac{\sqrt{p}}{p-1} \ln{p} < 1 \\ \Leftrightarrow & \ln{p} < \frac{p-1}{\sqrt{p}} \\ & \text{设 } q = \sqrt{p},\; q > 1 \\ \Leftrightarrow & 2\ln{q} < \frac{q^2 - 1}{q} \\ \Leftrightarrow & 2\ln{q} - \frac{q^2 - 1}{q} < 0 \end{aligned}

设 m(q) = 2\ln{q} - \frac{q^2 - 1}{q} ，

\begin{aligned} m'(q) &= 2 \cdot \frac{1}{q} - 1 - \frac{1}{q^2} \\ &= \frac{2q - q^2 - 1}{q^2} \\ &= -\frac{(q-1)^2}{q^2} < 0 \end{aligned} \begin{gathered} \therefore m(q) \text{ 在 } (1, +\infty) \text{ 上单调递减} \\ \because q \to 1,\; m(q) \to 0 \\ \therefore q > 1,\; m(q) < 0 \\ \therefore \frac{\sqrt{p}}{p-1} \ln{p} < 1 \\ \text{由 } (6) \text{ 得 } x_1 x_2 < 1 \end{gathered} \begin{aligned} & \text{由 } (5) \text{ 得 } h'(t) = \frac{x_1 x_2 - 1}{(x_1-1)(x_2-1)} \\ & \because x_1 < 1,\; x_2 > 1,\; x_1 x_2 < 1 \\ & \therefore h'(t) > 0 \\ & h(t) \text{ 在 } (2, +\infty) \text{ 上单调递增} \\ & \text{当 } t \to 2 \text{ 时：} x_1 \to 1,\; x_2 \to 1,\; \ln{x_1} \to 0 \\ & h(t) = x_2 + \ln{x_1} \to 1 \\ & \therefore t > 2 \text{ 时 } h(t) > 1 \end{aligned}

即证
